Wild Paws Rescue Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

Wild Paws Rescue is an animal rescue and care game developed and published by Cymru Pixels.

Information stored on your device

Wild Paws Rescue saves your game progress on your device. This includes your animals, buildings, park layout, coins and settings. The app also keeps a spare copy of this save in its own private storage on the same device, so progress isn't lost. Neither copy is sent anywhere.

Personal information

Wild Paws Rescue does not need an account. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information.

Advertising, purchases and analytics

The current version of Wild Paws Rescue has no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking.

Permissions

The app uses Android's standard internet permission only because its built-in game view needs it to start. The game does not use it to send or receive any information about you.

Information sharing

Cymru Pixels does not collect personal information through Wild Paws Rescue, and does not share any with others.

Save codes

In Settings you can copy a "save code" to back up your game. The code holds only your game progress. It stays wherever you choose to keep it, and Cymru Pixels never receives it.

Children's privacy

Wild Paws Rescue is suitable for children. It does not ask children for personal information, and contains no ads, chat or links to social media.

Data retention and deletion

Your game progress stays on your device until you remove it. Uninstalling the game, or using "Clear storage" for it in Android settings, deletes your progress.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that change any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, email cymrupixels@gmail.com.
